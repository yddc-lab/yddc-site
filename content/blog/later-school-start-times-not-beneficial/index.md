Key Points

- Later school start times have proved to provide many sleep and health benefits.

- Only recently have researchers asked if the benefits are uniform across social class and ethnicity.

- A recent large nationwide study showed that benefits were not found for some groups.

- Reduction in sleep health disparities will have to depend on more than just later school start times.
