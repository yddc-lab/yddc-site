Haiwei Liu is a 1st-year graduate student in the Applied Developmental Psychology program at Fordham University. She is interested in understanding how family dynamics, bicultural experiences, and everyday stressors shape the psychological and physical well-being of individuals from minority backgrounds. She is especially interested in how these experiences may accumulate over time and influence long-term health and development. Haiwei hopes her research can contribute to a better understanding of mental health and promote the well-being of diverse communities.
