Emma Eitmann is a senior at Fordham University's Rose Hill campus, majoring in psychology. She is passionate about understanding systemic barriers experienced by minority populations and exploring institutional belonging among individuals with disabilities. Her research interests include autism spectrum disorder (ASD), child and adolescent development, and the implementation of school-based interventions. Emma plans to pursue a Ph.D. in Clinical Psychology with a focus on ASD.
