Douglas Gelman (he/him/his) is a second-year master's student in the Applied Psychological Methods program, specializing in Tests and Measures. He is particularly interested in how stereotype threat, stereotype consciousness, and intergroup bias influence identity development and self-expression in adolescents and emerging adults. After completing his master's, he hopes to pursue a PhD in developmental psychology to explore these topics further and examine their long-term personal and interpersonal effects.
