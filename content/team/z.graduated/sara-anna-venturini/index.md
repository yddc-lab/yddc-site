Sara Anna Venturini is a fourth-year student at Fordham University's Rose Hill campus, pursuing a degree in Integrative Neuroscience with a concentration in Cellular and Molecular neuroscience on the Pre-Med track. She is also minoring in Psychology and completing an honors concentration in American Catholic Studies. After graduation, she hopes to attend medical school or physician assistant school. Outside of the lab, she is president of the Integrative Neuroscience Student Association (INSA), a member of the Sports Medicine team working with the Fordham football program, and an EMT with FUEMS.
