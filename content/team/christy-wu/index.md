Christy Wu (she/they) is a sophomore at Lincoln Center pursuing a B.S in Psychology. She is interested in studying developmental psychology and how trauma from childhood can affect adulthood, as well as multicultural psychology, focusing on understanding and addressing the unique mental health experiences of individuals from BIPOC communities. She hopes to pursue a career in child and adolescent psychology, focusing on the mental, emotional, and social development of children from infancy through adolescence.
