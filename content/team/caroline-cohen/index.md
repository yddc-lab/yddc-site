Caroline Cohen is a 1st-year graduate student in the Clinical Research Methods program at Fordham University. She graduated from Vanderbilt University with a major in Child Studies. She is interested in research on child and adolescent mental health, and understanding how early experiences and environmental factors shape psychological and behavioral outcomes across development.
