Gabrielle Diaz is a senior at Fordham University Rose Hill majoring in Psychology with a minor in the Biological Sciences. Her research interests include: how genetics influences the presentation of psychological disorders specifically ADHD and its presentation in young girls and women. Additionally, she is interested in how stress can affect neurological functioning. She is also interested in studying biopsychology and genetic neurodivergent and neurodegenerative disorders.
