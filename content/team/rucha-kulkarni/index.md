Rucha Kulkarni is a senior at Fordham University Rose Hill majoring in psychology and minoring in biology. Rucha's research interests include developmental and cross-cultural psychology; more specifically, she is interested in how healthcare disparities within minoritized populations and how racial and ethnic identity influences the academic experiences and outcomes of minority college students. She hopes to become a therapist and work to improve access to culturally competent mental health care for underrepresented communities. Outside of the lab, she is the Vice President of Fordham's Psychology Club, a representative for the Psychology department's Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Council, and co-founder of Mischief Managed, Fordham's Harry Potter club.
