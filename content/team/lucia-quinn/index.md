Lucia is a junior at Fordham University Lincoln Center, majoring in Psychology. Her research interests center around disparities within healthcare settings, and the impact of resilience in individuals and communities impacted by systemic healthcare disparities. She is also interested in how various psychopathologies affect individuals' access to adequate healthcare. Lucia plans to pursue a career as an emergency medicine physician. Outside of the lab, she enjoys spending her time at the bookstore.
