Charlene Weis is a sophomore at Fordham University's Rose Hill campus pursuing a BS in Biological Sciences. She's interested in how identity, environment, and systemic inequities shape mental health and well-being. At the YDDC Lab, she's excited to contribute to research highlighting the social and structural factors influencing development and health. After graduation, Charlene plans to pursue an MD/MPH and continue working at the intersection of clinical care, advocacy, and public health research.
