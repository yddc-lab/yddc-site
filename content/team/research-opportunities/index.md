Applications for Fall 2026 are open.

**[Fill out the YDDC Lab Undergraduate Research Assistant Application](https://forms.gle/vu2VX7j24M6WYA2i6)**

The Youth Development in Diverse Contexts (YDDC) Lab, directed by Dr. Tiffany Yip, is seeking undergraduates to assist with a longitudinal study of sleep, health, and academics during the college transition through graduation.

Research Assistants (RAs) will be integral to many aspects of the study, including working with participants for in-person lab visits while helping with data collection and management. RAs will also participate in weekly lab meetings and gain valuable research experience in psychology. We are specifically seeking students who are reliable, self-directed, and have strong communication skills. Prior research experience is a plus but not required.

Students must commit 10 hours per week for a minimum of 2 semesters and will be joining a large and diverse lab of undergraduates, graduate students, and postdoctoral fellows.

If you have further questions, please contact Angela Zhao: [azhao29@fordham.edu](mailto:azhao29@fordham.edu)
