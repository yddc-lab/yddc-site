Funded by an Interdisciplinary and a Faculty Research Grant (PI: Yip & Smith), this project explores the physiological embodiment of stress. This study includes a sample of over 700 diverse college students who completed a preliminary demographic survey, and a subsample of over 200 students who completed an experimental manipulation of stress in the research lab.

As part of the experimental study, participants were exposed to vignettes depicting varying degrees of stress. A psychological monitor (Biopac) assessed real-time heart-rate and skin response reactions to the vignettes. In addition, participants provided salivary and hair cortisol samples.

The College Stress Study explores how real-time responses to stress vary by sociodemographic and physiological variables, with the aim of unpacking health and academic disparities.
