title: "Youth Experiences Study (YES)"
subtitle: "PIs: Drs. Tiffany Yip & Nicole Shelton  |  2008 - 2010 "
excerpt: "A longitudinal study of 400 New York City adolescents examining ethnic identity, school context, and daily developmental outcomes."
weight: 9
---

This longitudinal study, funded by the National Institutes of Health, recruited 400 New York adolescent participants of diverse ethnic backgrounds from several high schools in the area.

Students completed three longer surveys each year, as well as a one-week mini-study. During that week, participants answered a nightly survey about their activities and mood that day. They were also beeped at random on phones distributed by the researchers five times during the day and prompted to answer questions about their feelings at that moment.

The daily diary design of the YES project allowed for investigation of how ethnic identity development, school context, and other factors affected everyday developmental outcomes, including mood and academic engagement.

**Grant number:** R01HD055436
