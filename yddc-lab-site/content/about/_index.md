## Our Lab Mission

Fordham's lab of Youth Development in Diverse Contexts (YDDC) examines the influence of various daily life experiences on developmental outcomes for youth and adolescents. We aim to understand the role of identity development, stress, and diverse contextual factors in determining psychosocial and academic trajectories for minority youth. YDDC research takes an ecosystemic approach, examining not only the role of racial experiences, but of many other factors on wellbeing, allowing for the development of a more holistic picture of youth functioning.

## Our Commitment to Ethical Research Practices

At the Youth Development in Diverse Contexts (YDDC) lab, we are committed to conducting ethical research that upholds the rights and privacy of all participants, especially those from marginalized communities. We acknowledge the historical injustices and exploitation that specific communities have faced in research studies. As a result, we prioritize the following principles to protect our participants' rights and interests:

1. Confidentiality of Participant Information
2. Ethical Oversight
3. Community Involvement
4. Implicit Bias Training and Organizational Race Equity Assessments
5. Staying True to Our Purpose
